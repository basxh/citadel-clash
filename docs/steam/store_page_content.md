```
Citadel Clash ist ein rasanter 3D-Strategie-Titel, der klassisches Base-Building mit modernem Gameplay verbindet. Inspiriert von Stronghold und Genre-Größen, fordern wir dich heraus: Baue unzerstörbare Festungen, verwalte komplexe Wirtschaftssysteme und führe epische Belagerungen – allein oder mit bis zu 7 Freunden im Multiplayer.
```

```
Citadel Clash is a fast-paced 3D strategy title that combines classic base-building with modern gameplay. Inspired by Stronghold and genre giants, we challenge you: Build unbreakable fortresses, manage complex economic systems, and lead epic sieges – solo or with up to 7 friends in multiplayer.

Every wall counts. Every archer can make the difference. Become the ruler of your own citadel and topple your enemies' fortresses.
```

### English:

2. ⚔️ **Tactical Sieges** - Destroy enemy fortifications with battering rams, siege towers and clever strategy
3. 💰 **Complex Economy** - Manage wood, stone, food and gold – every decision counts
4. 👥 **Multiplayer up to 8 Players** - Battle friends or forge alliances in online multiplayer
